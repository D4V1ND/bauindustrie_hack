LV Price Span

For the frontend team · what step 3 writes now for reference and none

Two methods changed. A reference item now gets a suggested price (Vorschlagspreis): the middle price of the related past items, in median, counted in the total. A none item still gets no price and confidence 0, but min and max now hold a span: the lowest and highest price of the 3 nearest past items in judged.

Every value on this page is real: P5, re-scored from its saved run with the new rules, without asking any AI model again.

Part 1 · none

No price, only a span

“Wasserhaltung Bach bis 150 l/s”: the judge found nothing the same. The 3 nearest past items span €350 to €2,150, so the screen shows that span and no price.

AICode The judge gives each candidate a verdict. Code takes the 3 it keeps in judged and writes their lowest and highest price.

What a none item holds

FieldTypeMeaning
judged[]array of 3 sourcesThe 3 nearest past items, best first, each with the judge's why. For none, every verdict is different.
min, maxnumber or nullThe span: the lowest and the highest unit_price in judged. Null when judged is empty.
countintegerHow many prices make the span, here 3.
median, meannullNo price. The item adds nothing to summary.total.
risk.confidence0Every item without a price has confidence 0.
risk.price_reasonstringSays the span in words, e.g. “… their prices span 350.00 to 2150.00.”

The span stays empty when the judge was not asked (--copy-only), when no past item has the same unit, or when the judge gave no answer: then judged is empty and min and max are null.

Read the span with care
  • The 3 items are the nearest by text and meaning, but the judge called each one a different thing. Their prices are a hint, not an estimate.
  • In H1 (a building priced from civil-engineering projects) the span of “Betonstahl B500B”, per tonne, is €0.95 to €182: from a binder and asphalt mixes. Show the 3 items next to the span so the estimator sees where it comes from.
Part 2 · reference

A suggested price

“Verkehrsregelung und -sicherung”: two related past items cost €1,200 and €3,800. Their middle, €2,500, is the suggested price.

AICode The judge calls the past items size_only or type_only. Code takes the median of their prices, as for similar.

What a reference item holds now

FieldTypeMeaning
mediannumberThe suggested price, per unit: the middle price of sources. Counted in summary.total.
min, max, mean, countnumber; integerRange, average and number of the related prices.
sources[]array of sourcesThe related past items, at most 3: the same kind, another size or type.
risk.confidenceinteger100 − score, now that the item has a price. Price risk stays 75, the highest of all priced methods, so confidence stays low: 35 here.
Part 3 · The whole LV

What it does to P5

33 of P5's 50 items are reference items. With a suggested price each, the project total rises from €10,952 to €153,267.46.

Code Totals and confidence are arithmetic on the saved items. No AI is involved.

What each method means for the screen

MethodWhenShow
copiedA past item is word for word identical.median as the price, marked exact.
similarThe judge called at least one past item the same thing.median as the price, with every source.
adjustedThe same thing exists in a smaller and a larger size. Code interpolates.median, both sources and calculation.
reference changedOnly related past items: the same kind, another size or type.median as the suggested price, the sources as “related, not the same”, and min–max as their range.
none changedThe judge called every candidate different.No price. Show min–max as a span with the 3 items of judged, and confidence 0. Never add it to a total.

Every method but none now has a price. None shows a span, never a price.

Bottom line

Show median whenever it is set. When it is null and min is set, show min–max as a span with the 3 items of judged, and keep it out of every total.
